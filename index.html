I am a Mechanical Engineering student passionate about aerospace, specializing in aerodynamics, propulsion systems, and Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD). My current research spans two thermo-fluid and energy systems projects: simulating shock-induced Richtmyer-Meshkov instability (RMI) in supersonic scramjet combustors using OpenFOAM and ANSYS Fluent to evaluate fuel mixing and total pressure recovery, and modeling centralized district cooling networks with stratified thermal energy storage in OpenModelica and MATLAB to optimize plant load-shifting and energy efficiency.

Effect of Fuel Type and Temperature on Shock-Induced Richtmyer-Meshkov Mixing of a Strut-Injected Fuel Layer in a Scramjet Combustor

Investigating shock-wave/fuel-jet interactions and baroclinic vorticity generation driving Richtmyer-Meshkov instability (RMI) in supersonic scramjet combustors. Evaluating how fuel species properties and injection temperatures govern molecular mixing efficiency and flameholding potential.

OpenFOAM ANSYS Fluent
Ongoing · Supervisor: Dr. Dewan Hasan Ahmed

Optimizing District Cooling System

District cooling (DC) is defined as a system that generates and distributes cold energy to meet the cooling needs of connected buildings, primarily using chilled water circulated through a network of pipes. It offers advantages over traditional air conditioning by providing better efficiency and a longer life cycle through a central chiller plant.
