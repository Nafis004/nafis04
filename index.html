I am a Mechanical Engineering student with a strong passion for aerospace and automobile engineering. My areas of interest include aerodynamics, propulsion systems, and vehicle dynamics, with a particular focus on Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD) analysis. I am enthusiastic about applying simulation and modeling tools to solve real-world engineering challenges and constantly seek opportunities to learn, research, and collaborate in these dynamic fields. My current research spans two computational thermo-fluid and energy systems projects: simulating shock-induced Richtmyer-Meshkov instability (RMI) in supersonic scramjet combustors using OpenFOAM and ANSYS Fluent to evaluate how fuel species and injection temperatures affect baroclinic vorticity, molecular mixing efficiency, and total pressure recovery downstream of a strut injector; and modeling centralized district cooling networks integrated with stratified thermal energy storage tanks in OpenModelica and MATLAB to optimize chiller plant load-shifting, thermal stratification, and overall system energy efficiency.

Seeking Fall 2027 / Spring 2028 Graduate Research Assistantships

Focused on high-speed compressible flows, shock-boundary layer & Richtmyer-Meshkov instabilities, and multi-physics thermo-fluid simulation (OpenFOAM, ANSYS Fluent, OpenModelica).
