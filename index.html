I am a Mechanical Engineering student passionate about aerospace , specializing in aerodynamics, propulsion systems, and Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD). My current research spans two thermo-fluid and energy systems projects: simulating shock-induced Richtmyer-Meshkov instability (RMI) insupersonic scramjet combustors using OpenFOAM and ANSYS Fluent to evaluate fuel mixing and total pressure recovery, and modeling centralized district cooling networks with stratified thermal energy storage in OpenModelica and MATLAB to optimize plant load-shifting and energy efficiency.
